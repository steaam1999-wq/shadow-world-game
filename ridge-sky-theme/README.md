# «Небо над хребтом» — theme for the CleanYourTV launcher

Made from the «Небо над хребтом» page (https://claude.ai/artifact/PMeZaWkk4gyjMHSWgatZzr): its sky,
layered ridges and pine forests, rendered without the clock (the launcher draws its own) at six moments
of the day and grouped by time of day:

| Part of day | Pictures |
|---|---|
| morning | dawn, morning |
| evening | day, sunset |
| night | blue hour, starry night |

A quiet rim focus, a light glass dock, icon-only buttons; the clock screensaver drifts over the same picture.

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File tools\theme.ps1 -TvHost 192.168.0.102 C:\path\to\ridge-sky-theme
```
